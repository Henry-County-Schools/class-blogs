## This is my article

Welcome to my seventh weekly blog post! If this is the first blog of mine that you read please know that these blogs that I publish each week are essentially a synopsis of what I've been doing in my computer science 2 class. This blog post is more than likely going to be my last blog post of the school year!

Throughout this past week I've been working on my 20% Project as mentioned in past blog posts. The only challenge I ran into this week was getting my secondary page and my home/main page to properly link to each other. My first response to this challenge was to look at the Next.JS docs, but the docs were rather vague in regards of my problem. I ended up getting help from my teacher, and he advised that I look at the Vercel/Next.JS repo on GitHub for a more specific solution. I ended up finding the solution, which was to create a separate folder within my app folder for my second page and put my file for my second page within the aforementioned folder, through the repo. Now I can finally focus on adding content and styling my second page.

As of currently, I am making adequate/decent progress on my 20% Project; however, the school year is about to end. I'm afraid that my 20% Project will not be completely finished when I leave early next week, but I am statisfied with how it is turning out.
