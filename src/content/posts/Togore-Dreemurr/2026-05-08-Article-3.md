# Week 3
After fixing the movement I added the sword, which was quickly changed to the pickaxe. The pickaxe would follow the mouse in a direction around the player.
```js
pickaxe.style.left =
    playerX +
    parseInt(window.getComputedStyle(player).width) / 2 +
    Math.cos(a) * r +
    "px";

pickaxe.style.top =
  playerY +
  parseInt(window.getComputedStyle(player).height) / 2 +
  Math.sin(a) * r +
  "px";
```
It took a while to center the pivot of the pickaxe onto the player, but I evenetually got it and moved onto making ore.
