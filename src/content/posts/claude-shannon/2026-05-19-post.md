Now-a days a lot of people have been relying on ai to do their work. According to articles Humans are becoming more reliable on ai materials, creating a "paradox of supervision" where reliance on autonomous tools leads to debt and a 47% drop in debugging capabilities. This shift towards delegation diminishes critical, friction-based learning and erodes the technical expertise needed to safely manage these systems. This lacks of hands-on practice actively triggers debt and skill atrophy, forcing coders into exhaustive, continuous oversight due to a permanent distrust of AI outputs. Ultimately, this overreliance degrades the very expertise needed for evaluation and strips away the foundational problem-solving clarity of professionals.
