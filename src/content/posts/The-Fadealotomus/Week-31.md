## 4/17/26, Week 31

Monday-Wednesday: I finished the entirety of the tutorial, and I've got an idea for my project! It's a simple "Cookie Clicker"-esque type of game, where you, instead of tapping a cookie, tap a white fedora (as an inside reference; however, for now, it will be an image of a cookie).

Thursday-Saturday: I was at a Band field-trip at Atlanta, GA!

Thursday was kinda boring; we were just getting there, having lunch + dinner on the way, and listening to the Atlanta Symphony. We got to the hotel by about 11:30 PM, and my roomates decided it would be funny to goof off and keep me up till about 2:30 AM. Not fun.
Friday was definitely better. We went to the World of Coca Cola, and I was able to get many pictures for my famlily to see (Probably around 35-45)! We also went to Six Flags, which was pretty fun. I got myself some nice sunglasses, many drinks (about 6 or 7 of them (I'm being honest) since they were free), and some fun rides along with! My legs were ONLY a bit weak by the end, but whatever. After that, we were back at the hotel, and luckily I got better sleep that night.
Saturday was the big day! We went to perform our two pieces and came back with a Gold rating! We were also invited to one of two locations for next year: Nashville, or Chicago, for our efforts. 
While we were waiting for the ceremony in which we figured that out, we were at the Georgia Aquarium. It wouldn't have been so bad if we weren't there for ***5 HOURS.*** However, I got a lil turtle there, and some pals decided to name 'em Terrence, so that's what I stuck with. Terrence the Turtle. 
After that, it was late, so we went back to the hotel. HOWEVER, since we did so well at our performance that day, curfew was pushed from 10:00 PM to 12:00! 🎉
I stayed up 'till maybe 11:00 before waking up again at around 12:05 for room inspections, then staying up till about 1:45.
Sunday was just the way back, in which we made it at around 3:15 PM. It was, again, very boring and long.
